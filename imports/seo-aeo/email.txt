Long-Form SEO/AEO Content Standards

For the new content, please use the attached style guide as the primary
framework, with the following requirements:

   -

   *Minimum 1,200 words per article/page.* The prompts below currently show
   300- or 600-word assignments; those numbers should be disregarded. Our new
   standard is *1,200+ words of substantive long-form content*.
   -

   Content needs to be *highly customized to the individual client*. We do
   not want generic articles where only the practice name, provider and city
   are swapped.
   -

   Research the client's website before writing. Understand their
   providers, credentials, treatments, technology, philosophy, locations,
   differentiators and terminology.
   -

   Include *custom provider quotes* whenever available. Quotes should sound
   natural and add genuine expertise or perspective. *Do not fabricate
   provider quotations.* If an approved quote is unavailable, flag an
   appropriate location where one could be added.
   -

   Incorporate *all relevant providers* from the practice into calls to
   action and appropriate sections rather than focusing exclusively on one
   individual unless the prompt specifically requires it.
   -

   Include multiple *internal links* to relevant treatment, provider,
   contact, booking and related educational pages on the client's website.
   -

   Include appropriate *authoritative outbound links*. The URLs
   accompanying the prompts below are starting references and should not
   necessarily be the only sources used.
   -

   Wherever applicable, incorporate the client's *Facebook and Instagram
   accounts* into the content strategy.
   -

   Incorporate *Google reviews/testimonials* where appropriate and useful.
   Reviews must be genuine and attributable; we should never manufacture
   review language.
   -

   Content should be *geographically relevant without keyword stuffing*.
   Neighborhoods and surrounding markets should appear naturally.
   -

   Use clear *H1/H2/H3 structure*, short readable paragraphs, FAQs where
   appropriate, and sections that directly answer common patient questions.
   -

   Content should support *traditional Google SEO and AI/AEO discovery
   simultaneously* through direct answers, strong entities, provider
   expertise, local relevance and useful factual context.
   -

   Every piece should conclude with a strong, customized *conversion-oriented
   CTA* directing the reader toward the appropriate provider(s),
   consultation, phone number or booking pathway.
   -

   Most importantly, *1,200 words is the minimum—not the objective by
   itself*. We want useful, authoritative content rather than filler simply
   to reach a word count.

Vikas, the overarching objective is to take the expertise and quality of
our best writers and make it *repeatable and scalable without allowing the
content to feel automated, generic or templated*.

The URLs above should be treated as *research starting points rather than
source material to rewrite*. Each writer should research the client's own
website first, understand the provider and practice, and then use
authoritative third-party sources to support the article.

We want every finished piece to look and read as though it was developed
specifically for that physician, injector or practice after thoroughly
understanding their business.

Please use the attached style guide together with the Dontage example as
the quality benchmark. The expectation is *1,200+ words of long-form,
localized, authoritative, highly customized content that strengthens
traditional organic search visibility while also giving AI/search engines
clear information about the practice, its providers, expertise, services
and geography.*
