# How Laser Skin Rejuvenation in Washington DC Can Improve Fine Lines, Sun Damage, and Skin Texture

Fine lines, brown spots, and rough skin often appear together, but they do not all need the same treatment. At DC Derm Docs in Washington, DC, [Fraxel Dual laser skin rejuvenation](https://dc-dermdocs.com/fraxel-dual/) can soften fine lines and improve uneven color and texture.

The treatment uses two wavelengths, or types of laser light, to address different concerns. It also stimulates collagen, the protein that supports skin structure; these changes involve healing time and may require repeat sessions.

## What Can Laser Resurfacing Improve?

The [American Academy of Dermatology’s guidance on sun-damaged skin](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/sun-damage-skin/wrinkles-sun-damage-can-be-treated/) identifies laser resurfacing as an option for wrinkles and age spots, while explaining that treatment cannot undo all accumulated sun damage.

The improvements depend on the concern being treated:

- **Fine lines:** Small creases may soften as treatment encourages collagen renewal. Expect improvement rather than the disappearance of every wrinkle.
- **Sun damage:** Selected brown spots and uneven pigmentation can become less noticeable. A smoother, more even complexion does not mean the skin is protected from future UV damage.
- **Skin texture:** Resurfacing can improve uneven texture and the appearance of some acne scars. Scar improvement means a change in appearance, rather than complete scar removal.

Laser resurfacing cannot lift significant sagging, such as a drooping jawline.

## How Fraxel Dual Addresses Lines, Spots, and Texture

Fraxel Dual is a nonablative fractional laser. “Nonablative” means it heats tissue without removing the skin’s outer layer; “fractional” means it treats tiny columns of tissue with untreated skin between them, rather than treating the entire surface at once.

The untreated skin between those columns supports healing while the heat encourages collagen renewal. The response within the skin can improve texture as well as the appearance of spots.

Ablative resurfacing removes outer skin tissue and usually requires more healing. Fraxel Dual’s nonablative approach generally gives subtler changes with a shorter recovery.

DC Derm Docs uses two wavelengths, identified by these numbers:

- **1550:** The practice uses this wavelength for fine lines, wrinkles, and acne scars.
- **1927:** This wavelength addresses pigment concerns such as brown spots and freckles.

The distinction matters if your main concern is a scattering of sunspots versus lines accompanied by acne scarring. The same device offers different treatment capabilities, but that does not make every setting appropriate for every face.

Fraxel can also address areas beyond the face. DC Derm Docs uses the 1927 wavelength on the neck, chest, hands, and arms, allowing treatment of exposed skin that facial resurfacing would leave untouched.

## How IPL Differs From Laser Resurfacing

DC Derm Docs also offers [IPL rejuvenation](https://dc-dermdocs.com/ipl-rejuvenation/) for concerns that include sun damage, age spots, and facial redness. IPL stands for intense pulsed light; it uses a broad range of light wavelengths and is not a laser.

IPL gives the practice another approach to spots and redness, while Fraxel Dual offers fractional resurfacing for concerns that include lines and uneven texture. These are different treatment options, so the choice should reflect your main concern, skin tone, and recovery needs.

## Skin Tone and Medical History Shape the Plan

A dermatologist needs to assess the skin before choosing a laser and its settings. [Mayo Clinic’s laser resurfacing overview](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/laser-resurfacing/about/pac-20385114) explains that treated skin can become darker or lighter, with a higher risk of lasting color changes in people with brown or Black skin.

Infection and scarring are also possible complications.

Experience treating your skin tone matters when weighing these risks. A recent tan, previous pigment changes, or a history of unusual scarring can also affect treatment suitability.

Preparation starts before treatment day. Before resurfacing, protect the area from sun exposure to reduce the risk of unwanted color changes afterward.

Share your medicines, recent skin treatments, and any history of cold sores. Resurfacing can reactivate the virus that causes cold sores, and some patients need preventive medicine.

Sun damage also deserves medical attention beyond appearance. A changing spot or a persistent scaly patch needs examination rather than an assumption that it is an ordinary sunspot; some sun-related growths are precancerous, and skin cancer can resemble an age spot.

## What Treatment and Recovery Can Feel Like

DC Derm Docs uses topical anesthesia for comfort during Fraxel Dual treatment. Numbing cream reduces discomfort, but laser treatment can still feel hot or sting.

In general, Fraxel appointments include skin preparation and time for numbing before the laser passes over the treatment area. A full-face Fraxel treatment may take about 20 to 30 minutes, apart from preparation; DC Derm Docs can confirm your appointment length.

Afterward, the skin may feel sunburned and look red or swollen. Redness and swelling can last several days, and dryness or peeling may follow; being able to work does not necessarily mean your skin will look ready for a photographed event.

Recovery varies with treatment intensity and your skin’s response. Follow your treatment’s care instructions for cleansing, moisturizer, makeup, and restarting products that can irritate healing skin. Do not pick at peeling or crusted areas.

## When Results Develop and How to Maintain Them

Nonablative resurfacing usually produces gradual improvement. Changes in skin tone and texture may be more noticeable than wrinkle smoothing, and a series of treatments over weeks or months may be needed.

Collagen renewal continues after the initial healing. A treatment series also means repeated recovery periods, which matters if your calendar has little flexibility. The concern being treated and healing between visits help determine the number and spacing of sessions.

Sun protection remains part of maintaining the improvement. Use sunscreen with an SPF of at least 30 and protect treated skin from sun exposure as directed; new UV damage can undermine results, and normal aging continues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Laser Skin Rejuvenation Remove Every Fine Line?

Laser resurfacing can soften fine lines, but it does not erase every wrinkle or correct significant sagging. Results vary, and a series of treatments may be needed.

### Does Fraxel Dual Treat Both Sunspots and Uneven Texture?

Yes. DC Derm Docs uses Fraxel Dual’s 1550 wavelength for concerns including fine lines and acne scars, and its 1927 wavelength for brown spots and freckles. The choice of settings depends on the skin and concern being treated.

### Is IPL the Same as a Laser?

No. IPL uses pulses of light across several wavelengths. DC Derm Docs offers it for concerns including age spots, sun damage, and facial redness; Fraxel Dual is a fractional laser used for resurfacing.

### Is There Downtime After Fraxel?

There can be redness, swelling, a sunburned feeling, dryness, and peeling. Recovery varies with treatment intensity and your skin, so leave room for healing when planning treatment around work or events.
