## Which Lines Can Botox Soften?

[Botox treatment](https://www.lamoneaesthetics.com/botox-bryn-mawr-pa/) temporarily reduces the muscle activity that creates expression lines. These often include the horizontal lines across the forehead, the vertical “11s” between the eyebrows, and crow’s feet beside the eyes.

For example, a crease that deepens when you frown has a movement-related component. Relaxing the muscles responsible can make that crease less pronounced, although the amount of improvement differs from person to person.

Botox does not add volume. If the concern is a hollow area or a loss of facial fullness, reducing muscle movement does not replace the missing volume; fillers serve a different purpose.

## What Happens Before and During Treatment?

A Botox consultation generally includes your goals, medical history, medications, and potential risks. The clinician determines whether treatment is appropriate and discusses the expected result before injections take place.

Prepare to share information that affects treatment planning:

- Prescription medicines, over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and supplements, including blood thinners.
- Any botulinum toxin treatment within the past four months, including the product used.
- Previous reactions to these injections, allergies, muscle or nerve conditions, or swallowing or breathing problems.
- Pregnancy, plans for pregnancy, or breastfeeding.

Botox is generally not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. A skin infection at a planned injection site or an allergy to the product can also prevent treatment; disclose these issues before injections are considered.

If you take prescribed blood thinners, speak with the prescribing clinician before making any change to reduce bruising.

During treatment, a clinician uses a very fine needle to place small amounts into selected muscles. The injections usually take less than 15 minutes, but that does not include all the time needed for discussion, assessment, or preparation.

Brief discomfort is possible, and ice or a topical anesthetic can help. Ask La-Mon’e which comfort options it offers, and confirm whether your first appointment is a consultation or a treatment visit when arranging your schedule.

## When Will Results Appear, and How Long Will They Last?

Botox is gradual rather than immediate. [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/botox/about/pac-20384658) reports that injections usually begin working in one to three days, while full results can take a week or more.

That distinction matters if you are planning around photographs or an event: the day of treatment is not the day to judge the finished result. Bruising may also remain visible while the effect develops.

For cosmetic wrinkles, improvement commonly lasts about three to four months. As the effect fades, muscle movement and the associated lines return, so maintaining the change generally involves repeat treatment.

A result review addresses how you responded to recent injections; a later maintenance treatment renews the effect as it wears off. Confirm whether La-Mon’e recommends a result review for you and what any adjustment would involve.

## Recovery and Side Effects to Plan For

Most people can return to everyday activities soon after treatment. Mayo Clinic advises avoiding rubbing or massaging the treated areas for 24 hours and not lying down for two to four hours after injections.

The American Academy of Dermatology advises waiting two hours before strenuous exercise, including workouts or heavy lifting. Follow any additional aftercare instructions from your clinician.

Possible side effects include soreness, swelling, bruising, and headache. Unwanted effects can include a drooping eyelid or an uneven eyebrow.

Contact the practice promptly about unexpected effects. Seek immediate medical help for trouble breathing, swallowing, or speaking after treatment; these can signal a serious reaction.
