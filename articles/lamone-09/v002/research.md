## Medical Evidence

- [ASPS procedure](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/dermal-fillers/procedure): Facial assessment, cleansing, anesthetic measures, injections, and evaluation during treatment. Supports the appointment sequence without promising painless treatment or a fixed duration.
- [ASPS recovery](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/dermal-fillers/recovery): Initial appearance can include overfilling from swelling, bruising, tenderness, and variable recovery. Supports waiting for a settled assessment rather than judging the immediate appearance.
- [FDA filler guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers): Temporary effects, product/site specificity, removal risks, common reactions, rare vascular injury with serious tissue/vision consequences, and immediate attention for unusual pain, vision changes, or white/gray/blue skin. Supports risk and correction discussion. No assurance that dissolving resolves every complication.
- [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22133-lip-fillers): Consultation health history includes allergy and cold-sore history. [Lip flip explanation](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22362-lip-flip) distinguishes muscle relaxation from adding volume. These are limited background facts, not the source of the whole article.
