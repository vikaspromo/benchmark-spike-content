## Medical Evidence

- [AAD FAQs](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/hair-removal/laser-hair-removal-faqs): Results can last months or years; maintenance may be needed. Repeated sessions, temporary redness/swelling, protective eyewear, burns, scarring, and pigment changes support the results and safety sections. [AAD overview](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/hair-removal/laser-hair-removal-overview) supports hormonal persistence of facial hair.
- [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/laser-hair-removal/about/pac-20394555): Pigment-targeted heating; variable hair-growth phases; influence of skin tone and hair color; poorer response of light/gray/white hair; tanning risk; avoiding waxing/plucking, shaving instructions, and medical assessment. Supports suitability and preparation. No blanket guarantee is repeated.
- [AAD preparation](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/hair-removal/laser-hair-removal-preparation): Medicines, sun exposure, scarring history, and skin assessment support pre-treatment discussion. The draft does not advise stopping prescribed medication.
