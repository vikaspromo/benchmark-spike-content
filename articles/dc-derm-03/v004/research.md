## Independent Medical Evidence and Claim Use

- [AAD FAQs](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/hair-removal/laser-hair-removal-faqs), procedure, aftercare, results, timing, and safety: cleaned skin; protective eyewear for everyone; warm pinprick/snapping sensation; upper lip minutes, back/legs potentially over an hour; redness/swelling; cool compress; most return to routine activities. Hair/skin/laser/area affect outcomes; repeat and maintenance visits can be required. Supports visit and recovery passages and variability, not a fixed practice timetable. Article includes this link.
- [AAD Preparation](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/hair-removal/laser-hair-removal-preparation), consultation and information sections: consultation before treatment; disclose recent tan, scarring/keloids, herpes/cold sores, isotretinoin and other medicines. Avoid tanning and sunless tanners; broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen. Supports preparation and disclosure list. Article does not declare those histories absolute contraindications.
- [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/laser-hair-removal/about/pac-20394555), reviewed July 21, 2026: melanin absorbs light, heat damages follicles; not guaranteed permanent; shedding days/weeks; multiple sessions weeks apart; avoid tattoos, eyelids/eyebrows; risks include burns, pigment changes, blistering, scarring. Shave before treatment and stop waxing/plucking/electrolysis. Supports mechanisms, preparation, anatomical limits, and risks. Article links here. Advice to contact the practice for worsening reaction is prudent producing-agent advice, not a claimed practice policy.
- [Penn Medicine](https://www.pennmedicine.org/treatments/laser-hair-removal), candidacy and longevity: dark hair responds best; blond/white/gray and peach fuzz respond less; darker skin candidates need experienced care because melanin raises burn risk when improperly treated. Long-lasting results may need touch-ups and regrowth is often lighter/thinner. Supports hair/skin distinctions, not Penn's comparative electrolysis claim.
- [ASDS Hair Removal](https://www.asds.net/skin-experts/skin-conditions/hair-removal), explanatory and treatment sections: light targets follicle pigment; settings depend on skin, hair color/thickness, and area; clinician experience with specific skin type matters; lasting results differ. Supports individualized parameters and skin-tone discussion. No article claims all wavelengths equally suit every person.
- [AAD Overview](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/hair-removal/laser-hair-removal-overview/): hormones prevent permanent results on a woman's face. Supports the facial-hair qualification; article uses cautious broader wording.
- [Healthline supplied reference](https://www.healthline.com/health/laser-hair-removal), updated June 16, 2018: covers mechanism, preparation, sessions, side effects, costs. Used as the assignment starting reference and question map, not as sole clinical authority; dated cost and satisfaction figures were not used.
