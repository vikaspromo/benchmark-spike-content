## Independent Medical Evidence

- [Cleveland Clinic: Lip Flip](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22362-lip-flip), medically reviewed February 4, 2022: Overview distinguishes upper-lip muscle relaxation/eversion from HA volume. Risks section supports excessive relaxation with drinking/speech problems and no immediate reversal. Recovery supports allowing time before events. Interpretation: a change in visible surface cannot substitute for added volume; function matters for daily activities. No broad claim that flips always look more natural is adopted.
- [Cleveland Clinic: Lip Fillers](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22133-lip-fillers), medically reviewed December 1, 2021: Overview supports modified HA, selected lip areas, shape and uneven-size goals. Consultation supports medical history, medications, allergies, and prior assessment; eligibility excludes active oral infection. Recovery supports swelling obscuring the settled result. Risks/benefits supports possible HA dissolution with hyaluronidase. This page's general 12–18-month estimate is not transferred to Estar's named formulation.
- [AAD: Fillers FAQs](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/wrinkles/fillers-faqs): “Will I look natural?” supports expert placement and conservative treatment rather than overfilling. Results section supports immediate HA fullness. Temporary side effects include redness, swelling, tenderness, and bruising. Interpretation: natural appearance is a treatment-planning objective, not proof that flip is inherently superior. Most patients resume everyday activities; this does not guarantee an unmarked appearance.
- [ASPS: Dermal Filler Procedure](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/dermal-fillers/procedure): Steps support facial assessment, cleansing, comfort measures, injections, and ongoing evaluation. Article explicitly presents this as general filler sequence, not Estar's confirmed same-day visit protocol.
- [FDA: Juvéderm Ultra XC lip patient labeling](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf5/P050047S044C.pdf): Benefits supports temporary fullness and improvement through one year for most study subjects. Procedure/aftercare supports discomfort despite lidocaine and topical options. Not a guarantee or proof of identical duration for other formulations.
- [FDA: Dermal Fillers](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers): Removal section states interventions carry risks. Blood-vessel risk section supports low-frequency serious tissue/vision harm; patient advice supports immediate attention for unusual pain, vision changes, or white/gray/blue skin. FDA cautions products have specific approved sites; no filler-family-wide lip indication is inferred.
