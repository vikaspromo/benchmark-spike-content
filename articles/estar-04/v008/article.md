# What Is the Difference Between a Lip Flip and Lip Filler?

A lip flip relaxes the muscle around your upper lip so more of the lip shows. Lip filler adds volume with an injectable gel, which can also refine the shape of your upper or lower lip.

The choice depends on what you want to change: a lip that rolls inward when you smile, lips that look thin even at rest, or a border that could use more definition.

## What a Lip Flip Can Change

During a [Botox lip flip](https://estarmedspa.com/services/botox-lip-flip-olney-maryland/), small injections relax part of the muscle surrounding the mouth. This lets the upper lip turn slightly outward, revealing more of its pink surface without adding material to the lip itself.

For example, you may like your lip size when your face is relaxed but notice that your upper lip nearly disappears in a broad smile. A lip flip may help that upper lip stay more visible when you smile.

Its effect is modest: it cannot supply the added fullness of filler or enlarge a thin lower lip. Botox used for a lip flip is an off-label treatment, meaning this particular use is outside its FDA-approved uses.

## What Lip Filler Can Change

[Lip filler](https://estarmedspa.com/services/lip-filler-in-olney-maryland/) places gel into the lips to increase fullness or adjust their contours. Hyaluronic acid, often shortened to HA, is a substance found naturally in the body; injectable HA fillers are manufactured gels.

Filler can address a different set of goals: restoring lost volume, balancing upper and lower lip proportions, or defining the lip border. Placement and product choice affect which change is possible, so fuller lips do not have to mean a dramatic increase in size.

Estar's [Juvéderm treatment options](https://estarmedspa.com/services/juvederm-olney-maryland/) include Ultra XC for lip volume and definition and Volbella XC for subtle enhancement and vertical lip lines.

If the main concern is how your upper lip curls inward, adding gel alone does not perform the muscle-relaxing action of a flip. A clinician may consider both treatments when volume and movement each contribute.

Combining them requires an individual plan. The FDA notes that the safety of filler used with Botox has not been evaluated in controlled clinical studies.

## Results, Timing, and Maintenance Costs

A lip flip develops gradually. Estar advises that a change may appear within the first week, with the full effect taking 10 to 14 days.

Filler adds fullness right away, but early swelling can make the lips look larger than the settled result. Cleveland Clinic notes that swelling often improves within 24 to 48 hours but can take up to a week.

Lip flips typically last two to five months, according to [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22362-lip-flip). At Estar, lip filler is expected to last six to 12 months on average, depending on the product.

These are planning ranges, not expiration dates. Product choice and individual response affect how long the result lasts, and maintenance is needed if you want to keep the effect.

A lip flip generally costs less per session than filler, but its shorter duration can mean more frequent visits. Compare the proposed treatment amount, expected maintenance, and total cost rather than assume the lower starting price makes it the better value.

## How Do Recovery and Risks Compare?

Both treatments can cause bruising, swelling, and tenderness. A lip flip usually involves less swelling than filler, but relaxing a muscle used in daily activities creates a different concern: temporary trouble drinking, speaking, or keeping the lips closed.

A subtle cosmetic result can still affect lip function.

Estar's aftercare differs by treatment:

- After a lip flip, avoid lip products for three to four days, pressure on the lips, and sleeping face down.
- After filler, avoid lip products for at least 24 hours and wait 24 to 48 hours before exercise.

Follow the instructions given for your own treatment rather than use one procedure's aftercare for the other.

Filler also carries a rare but serious risk if it enters a blood vessel: tissue damage, blindness, or stroke. The [FDA's dermal filler guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers) explains these risks and recommends a licensed clinician trained in anatomy and complication management.

Seek immediate medical attention for unusual pain, white, gray, or blue skin, or vision changes after filler. Trouble breathing or swallowing after botulinum toxin injections also requires immediate medical attention.

## Can Either Treatment Be Undone?

A lip flip cannot be dissolved. If you dislike the appearance or muscle effects, you generally need to wait for the Botox to wear off.

HA filler can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase when a clinician determines that it is appropriate. Dissolving is another medical treatment, with its own risks, rather than a guaranteed reset that makes filler risk-free.
