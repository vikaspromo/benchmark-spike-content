## Medical Evidence

- [Cleveland Clinic lip flip](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22362-lip-flip): Muscle relaxation changes upper-lip position without volume; delayed effect; possible excessive relaxation affecting drinking/speech; the effect cannot be immediately reversed. The article does not repeat the broad claim that flips generally look more natural.
- [Cleveland Clinic lip filler](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22133-lip-fillers): HA volume and shape changes, initial fullness and swelling, variable lasting effect, rare blood-vessel injury, and potential dissolution of HA. Supports the comparison without guaranteed outcome or easy-reversal promises.
- [FDA filler guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers): Serious vascular consequences and risks of correction support the limited risk discussion. The article does not recommend a combined plan or invent combination safety.
