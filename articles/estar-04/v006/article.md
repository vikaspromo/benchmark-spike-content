# What Is the Difference Between a Lip Flip and Lip Filler?

A lip flip changes how the upper lip sits and moves; lip filler adds volume and can change lip shape. Both can make lips appear fuller, but they reach that result in different ways and involve different commitments.

At Estar MedSpa & Innovative Health Center in Olney, MD, both treatments are available. The useful starting point is the change you want to see: more upper lip showing when you smile, more fullness, a clearer border, or better balance between your lips.

## A Lip Flip Reveals Lip Tissue; Filler Adds Fullness

A [Botox lip flip](https://estarmedspa.com/services/botox-lip-flip-olney-maryland/) uses small injections of a muscle-relaxing medicine around the upper lip. As the muscle relaxes, the lip can turn slightly outward so more of its surface shows.

That can help when the upper lip curls inward during a smile. The change comes from the way existing tissue is displayed, so a lip flip cannot supply the added volume of a filler injection.

[Lip filler](https://estarmedspa.com/services/lip-filler-in-olney-maryland/) places gel into the lips to increase fullness or refine shape. Hyaluronic acid fillers are commonly used, and treatment can address the upper lip, lower lip, or both according to the plan.

The difference matters when describing your goal. A lip that looks small mainly during smiling raises a different treatment question from lips that look thin at rest; adding material and changing muscle movement are separate choices.

Filler can also improve differences in shape or size between the lips. A lip flip focuses on the effect of muscle relaxation, rather than providing the same control over volume and borders.

## Match the Treatment to the Feature You Want to Change

Several concerns can sound like “I want fuller lips” but call for different conversations:

- **Your upper lip disappears when you smile:** A lip flip may make more upper lip visible without increasing its volume. The likely change is subtle.
- **You want more fullness in one or both lips:** Filler can add volume where it is needed. The amount should reflect your existing proportions and desired result.
- **You want a clearer outline or a change in shape:** Filler can refine these features through product selection and placement. A lip flip does not replace that shaping role.
- **You want both added fullness and more upper-lip show:** Filler can address thin lips at rest, while a lip flip can address an upper lip that curls inward when you smile. A clinician assesses whether both changes are needed.

A photograph at rest can miss what happens when you smile or speak. Your description should include when you notice the concern, not just how large you want the lips to look in a still image.

## Results Arrive and Fade on Different Timelines

A lip flip develops gradually. Estar describes a visible change during the first week, with the full effect taking 10 to 14 days; an immediate post-injection photograph will not show the completed result.

The practice describes an average duration of three to four months. Cleveland Clinic’s [lip flip overview](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22362-lip-flip) gives a broader two- to five-month range, which illustrates why your treatment should not be planned around a guaranteed end date.

Filler creates an immediate increase in volume, but swelling can temporarily distort the appearance. Assessing the settled shape is different from assessing the first day’s fullness.

Estar gives a typical six- to 12-month duration for lip filler, depending on the brand and formulation. The effect is temporary, and future treatment may be needed if you want to maintain the change.

A shorter-lasting treatment is not necessarily a better trial for someone who wants volume. A lip flip can fade sooner while still failing to produce the specific change that filler is meant to provide.

Likewise, a longer duration does not make filler the better choice for every smile concern. Compare the result you want, the maintenance involved, and how you feel about each treatment’s risks before deciding.

## Comfort and Recovery at Estar

Estar’s lip flip service includes the option of numbing cream or a cold compress for people who are concerned about discomfort. The injection process is described as taking 10 to 20 minutes, followed by 15 minutes of observation for immediate reactions.

If you choose numbing, the waiting time adds to the visit. A short injection procedure should not be confused with the entire appointment, especially when an assessment is also needed.

For lip filler, Estar describes numbing cream before injections and a short observation period afterward. Brief pinching or pressure may still occur.

Estar describes most lip filler appointments as an hour or less, with longer visits possible for new patients or more complex goals. Numbing can take 15 to 30 minutes to work before the injections begin.

Most people can resume ordinary activities after either treatment. Estar advises avoiding pressure on the lips after a flip and pausing exercise for 24 to 48 hours after filler.

Swelling, bruising, and tenderness can follow injections. Allow time for those changes and for the intended result to develop if you are planning around photographs or a special occasion.

## The Risks and Reversal Options Are Different

A lip flip can relax the muscles too much, making drinking, speaking, or keeping liquid in the mouth harder. Because the treatment affects function as well as appearance, this possibility matters even when only a small cosmetic change is planned.

The effect cannot be immediately reversed; you generally need to wait for the medicine to wear off. Seek urgent medical care for difficulty breathing or swallowing after botulinum toxin treatment.

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase when medically appropriate. That option is not a guarantee of effortless reversal, and removing or reducing filler can require additional treatment.

The [FDA explains the risks of dermal fillers](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers), including rare injection into a blood vessel. A blockage can damage tissue and, in serious cases, cause vision loss or stroke.

Unusual pain, vision changes, or white, gray, or blue skin near the injection site require immediate medical attention. Those symptoms should not be treated as ordinary bruising while you wait for a scheduled review.

Before either procedure, disclose medicines, allergies, and health conditions, including an infection around the mouth. Your medical history can affect suitability even when the cosmetic goal seems modest.
