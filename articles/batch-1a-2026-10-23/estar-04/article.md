# What Is the Difference Between a Lip Flip and Lip Filler?

A lip flip changes how the upper lip sits; lip filler adds volume. A flip uses Botox to relax muscle near the upper lip so more of the lip shows, while filler places gel in the lips to increase fullness or refine their shape.

That difference matters more than whether you want a subtle result. Estar MedSpa & Innovative Health Center in Olney, MD, offers both options, and a small change in lip shape may call for a different treatment than a small increase in volume.

## What Does a Lip Flip Change?

With a [Botox lip flip](https://estarmedspa.com/services/botox-lip-flip-olney-maryland/), the upper lip rolls slightly outward as the treated muscle relaxes. The treatment reveals more of your existing lip without adding volume.

This may suit someone whose upper lip becomes less visible when smiling or who wants a little more upper-lip show without filler. It can also reduce gum exposure in some smiles, but it does not make the lower lip fuller.

Botox used for a lip flip is off-label, meaning this specific use is outside its FDA-approved indications. The choice still needs a clinician's assessment of your lip movement, health, and goals.

## Does Lip Filler Always Mean a Dramatic Change?

[Lip filler](https://estarmedspa.com/services/lip-filler-in-olney-maryland/) can add modest fullness, define the lip border, or improve balance between the upper and lower lips. Hyaluronic acid, or HA, is the water-binding substance used in many lip filler gels.

The amount and placement influence the result. Wanting a natural appearance does not, by itself, make a lip flip the better choice; someone seeking more fullness may need filler even if the desired change is small.

Lip fullness and fine lines around the lips are different concerns. A product used elsewhere on the face is not automatically suitable for the lips.

## Can You Use a Lip Flip and Filler Together?

Sometimes the goals overlap. A person may want added fullness in both lips and more of the upper lip to show when smiling; filler supplies volume, while a flip addresses the way the upper lip sits.

Combining treatments requires an individual assessment, and Estar's injector can determine whether one or both approaches fit your anatomy. The FDA notes that filler safety in combination with Botox or other treatments has not been evaluated in a controlled clinical study.

## When Will Results Appear, and How Long Will They Last?

A lip flip takes time to develop. Cleveland Clinic's [lip flip guide](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22362-lip-flip) describes visible change around one week and effects that generally last two to five months.

Filler adds visible fullness right away, although swelling can obscure the result at first. You should allow time for healing before deciding whether the lips need more volume.

Estar gives a general lip filler estimate of six to 12 months, depending on the product. For its Juvéderm lip options, the manufacturer reports results lasting up to one year with optimal treatment.

A lower initial price for a flip may come with more frequent visits, while longer-lasting filler involves a different upfront cost and commitment.

## What Are the Appointment and Recovery Like at Estar?

For lip flips, Estar offers numbing cream or a cold compress for people concerned about discomfort. The injection portion typically takes 10 to 20 minutes, followed by 15 minutes of observation; time for numbing can make the full visit longer.

For filler, Estar applies numbing cream before lip injections. You may still feel a pinch or pressure.

Estar advises avoiding lip products for three to four days after a flip, as well as avoiding pressure on the lips and sleeping face down.

Filler may cause swelling, bruising, and tenderness. Swelling often improves within 24 to 48 hours but may last up to a week, so your appearance immediately after injections does not show the settled result.

Allow at least two weeks before an important event for either treatment, as Cleveland Clinic recommends. Follow your injector's instructions for your product and treatment.

## What Are the Risks, and Can You Change Your Mind?

A lip flip can weaken the muscles around the mouth too much, making drinking or speaking harder. It cannot be dissolved, so unwanted effects must wear off.

HA filler can be reduced with an enzyme called hyaluronidase. That requires another medical treatment, and removal has its own risks; “dissolvable” should not be treated as an instant, risk-free reset.

The [FDA's filler safety guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers) explains a rare but serious risk: filler entering a blood vessel can block blood flow and cause tissue damage, blindness, or stroke. Seek immediate medical attention for unusual pain, vision changes, or white, gray, or blue skin near the injection site.

After botulinum toxin injections, trouble breathing, swallowing, or speaking also needs immediate medical attention. Before either procedure, discuss medicines, allergies, prior injections, pregnancy or breastfeeding, and any cold sores or active infection with the clinician.
