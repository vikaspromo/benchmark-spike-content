## What Can Lip Filler Change?

La-Mon’e uses hyaluronic acid fillers, gels that add volume where more fullness or contour is wanted. Hyaluronic acid is a substance that occurs naturally in the body and holds water.

You might want a more defined outline without making both lips much larger. Someone else might want to restore fullness or make an uneven shape less noticeable; those are different goals, even though both involve lip filler.

The amount of filler is part of the plan, not a target every first-time patient must reach. Starting with thin lips does not mean a large increase will suit your face, and a photograph of someone else's lips cannot establish the right amount for yours.

The [American Academy of Dermatology's filler guide](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/wrinkles/fillers-faqs) favors a conservative approach for natural-looking results. La-Mon’e's team places facial filler gradually, using conservative amounts to build the result.

## How Should You Prepare for the Visit?

Bring the information your clinician needs to review your health:

- Medical conditions, allergies, bleeding problems, and any history of cold sores.
- All medicines and supplements, including products you take without a prescription.
- Previous cosmetic injections or implants, even if another part of your face was treated.

These details help the clinician assess suitability and risks. Do not stop a prescribed medicine on your own to prepare for an elective cosmetic treatment; arrange any changes with the clinician responsible for that medicine.

Tell the practice if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. The FDA states that filler safety is unknown during pregnancy and breastfeeding and in people younger than 22; FDA-approved uses are for adults 22 and older.

La-Mon’e advises avoiding alcohol before and after lip filler and avoiding excess pressure on the treated area afterward. Get the instructions that apply to your appointment before treatment, so you can plan around them rather than try to remember everything while your lips are tender.

## What Do the Injections Feel Like?

Your clinician may use several injections to place the filler. Sensations can include pressure, pinching, or stinging, even with measures to reduce discomfort.

La-Mon’e uses numbing cream and filler containing lidocaine to help keep lip treatment comfortable. Tell your clinician about any past reaction to numbing medicine before treatment begins.

Most filler injection treatments take about 15 to 30 minutes, according to the AAD. That is a general treatment estimate, not the length of a first appointment at La-Mon’e: consultation, preparation, and care instructions require time too.

The selected product also matters. Before receiving it, review the product name and patient information with your clinician, including its approved uses and risks; hyaluronic acid fillers are not all interchangeable.

## When Will Your Lips Look Settled?

Hyaluronic acid filler adds visible volume immediately, but the first mirror check includes swelling as well as filler. Early fullness is not a reliable picture of the settled result, so leave room for recovery before judging the size or symmetry.

Temporary swelling, tenderness, and bruising can occur. The AAD reports that minor injection-site effects often clear within seven to 14 days, sometimes sooner; La-Mon’e's lip-care guidance cautions that settling can take several weeks.

Returning to everyday activities and looking ready for an important photograph are different expectations. If you have an event coming up, discuss its date before choosing a treatment date rather than count on bruising being gone by a particular day.

The AAD advises waiting until the next day for strenuous exercise. Follow your own aftercare instructions, including guidance about pressure or massage; do not press on an uneven area to try to reshape it yourself.

## How Long Does Filler Last, and Can It Be Adjusted?

Many hyaluronic acid fillers have effects lasting roughly six to 12 months. Duration depends on the product and treatment area, so your own plan may differ; repeat treatment adds to the cost if you want to maintain the added fullness.

Hyaluronic acid filler can be reduced with an enzyme treatment. La-Mon’e offers filler dissolving as a separate service and notes that more than one session may be needed.

Dissolving has its own risks and needs clinical assessment. A temporary filler still involves a medical commitment, including treatment and recovery if you want it reduced; it is not a change to make on the assumption that removal will be effortless.

## Which Symptoms Need Medical Attention?

Filler is a medical procedure with risks beyond short-term swelling. Infection, persistent lumps, and allergic reactions can occur; rare injection into a blood vessel can damage tissue or cause blindness or stroke.

The [FDA's dermal filler safety guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers) calls for immediate medical attention for unusual pain, vision changes, or skin turning white, gray, or blue near an injection site. Sudden trouble speaking or weakness also needs emergency care; do not wait for a routine follow-up.
