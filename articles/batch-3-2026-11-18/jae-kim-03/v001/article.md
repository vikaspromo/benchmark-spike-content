## How Facial Balancing Changes the Way Your Features Fit Together

The cheeks frame the middle of the face. When they lose fullness, the transition from the cheek to the area beside the mouth can look sharper. Restoring some of that curve can soften the transition, even if a crease remains.

A chin that sits farther back is a different concern. From the side, adding projection to the chin can change how it lines up with the lower lip and jaw. That can change your profile even if you haven’t lost volume with age.

If the curve of your cheek has flattened, filling a line beside the mouth may soften the crease while leaving the cheek flat. Seeing the cheek and crease together helps you decide which change matters more to you.

The [American Society of Plastic Surgeons distinguishes adding facial volume from lifting tissue](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/cosmetic-procedures/dermal-fillers). If loose skin is causing jowls or a change in your neck, adding fullness can leave that sagging largely unchanged.

## Why the Type of Filler Matters

Hyaluronic acid filler is a gel that adds volume where it is placed. Some gels support a more defined shape, while others are made to move more easily in areas that shift as you smile or speak.

A firmer gel may help define the chin. A more flexible gel may be better suited to a fold near the mouth, where your face moves often.

YAB offers products from the RHA and Restylane collections, which both contain hyaluronic acid. It also offers Radiesse, a calcium-based filler that can add structure and support collagen production over time.

FDA approvals differ by product and treatment area. At the consultation, your injector can explain which formulation fits the change you want and why.

The effect is temporary, and its duration depends on the product and where it was used. If you’re planning around an event, leave room for bruising or swelling to settle.

Bruising and swelling are common. A rare but serious problem occurs if filler enters a blood vessel, which can damage the skin, affect vision, or cause a stroke.

The [FDA advises getting medical help immediately](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers) if you notice unusual pain, vision changes, or skin that turns white, gray, or blue near the injection site during or shortly after treatment.
