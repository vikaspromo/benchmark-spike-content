## What Can Facial Balancing With Filler Change?

As you age, the fat beneath your skin can shift or lose volume, and the bone underneath can change shape. That can leave the middle of your face looking flatter, even if the change you noticed first was a deeper crease near your mouth.

If your cheek has lost fullness, adding volume there can restore some of its curve. A filler placed in the crease itself can soften the fold. How much of the crease to soften depends on how it fits with the contours around it.

Chin filler can bring the outline of a chin forward. From the front, your chin may already look centered. From the side, its position relative to your lips helps the injector see whether a little more projection would suit your profile.

Those examples involve two different goals. Some people want to bring back a contour they’ve lost, while others want to refine a feature that has always looked that way. Either goal can call for treating a single area.

## When Will You See a Result?

If you proceed with treatment, the injector may numb the area before giving the injections. Hyaluronic acid filler usually changes the shape right away, and swelling can make that first change look bigger than it will after healing.

For facial contouring, the practice advises allowing a week or two for the change to settle. For some patients, the plan may take more than one visit. Waiting until swelling subsides gives you a clearer view of how the new contour fits with the rest of your face before deciding about additional filler.

Hyaluronic acid filler wears off over time. How long it lasts depends on the product and the area treated. If you like the result and want to maintain it, you can decide about another treatment as the effect fades.

## What Should You Know About Recovery and Risks?

Tenderness, swelling, and bruising can develop soon after injections, so leave time for visible swelling or bruising to fade before an event. The American Academy of Dermatology recommends [waiting until the next day for strenuous exercise](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/wrinkles/fillers-faqs).

In rare cases, filler can enter a blood vessel and block blood flow, causing tissue damage, vision loss, or stroke. Get medical help right away for unusual pain, a change in vision, skin near the injection that turns white, gray, or blue, or other [warning signs the FDA lists](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers?board=8).
