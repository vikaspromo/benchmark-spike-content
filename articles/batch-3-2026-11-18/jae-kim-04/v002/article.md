# Lip Filler in Fairfax, VA: What Are the Best Options for Natural-Looking Lip Enhancement?

Natural-looking lips can mean a clearer outline, a little more fullness, or a less noticeable difference between the two sides. A carefully placed hyaluronic acid gel can address each of these goals, with the amount and placement depending on what you want to change.

At You Are Beautiful (YAB) Aesthetic Institute, small amounts of gel can be placed to define the lip edge and add subtle fullness in proportion with the face. That approach gives you a starting point for planning [**lip filler in Fairfax, VA**](https://www.jaekimmd.com/lip-filler-fairfax-virginia/).

## Choose the Change You Want to See

If you like the size of your lips but miss a crisp outline, filler can define the edge or bring out the Cupid’s bow, the double curve at the center of the upper lip. That can make the shape easier to see without aiming for a much fuller mouth.

If your lips look thinner than they used to, filler placed within the lip can restore volume. That can change how far the lip projects in profile, so it helps to consider how much fullness you want from the side as well as from the front.

For a slight difference from side to side, filler can add volume where one side is less full. If the difference only appears when you smile, muscle movement may play a role, and filler doesn’t change how your muscles move.

YAB’s approach of placing small deposits of gel allows for plans that focus on specific features. If you like your lower lip and want more definition up top, for example, the plan can focus on the upper lip.

## Compare Two Lip Filler Options at YAB

[Two gels in YAB’s filler portfolio](https://www.jaekimmd.com/dermal-fillers/), Restylane Kysse and RHA 3, are FDA-approved for lip enhancement in adults 22 and older. Both use a gel made of hyaluronic acid, a substance already found in the body.

- **Restylane Kysse** adds lip fullness and is also approved to treat lines around the upper lip. That may matter if you’re concerned about both a thinner upper lip and fine lines in the skin above it.
- **RHA 3** adds lip fullness and is approved for use in the body and border of the lip and at the corners of the mouth for lip enhancement. At YAB, it’s part of the RHA collection, which is designed to flex with facial movement.

For a first treatment aimed at a subtle change, a modest starting amount leaves room to decide whether you want more after swelling goes down. The amount in a syringe can exceed what you need for a small change.

## When a Lip Flip Makes More Sense

If your upper lip looks close to the way you want at rest but rolls inward when you smile, YAB also offers a [Botox lip flip](https://www.jaekimmd.com/botox-for-lip-flip/). Botox relaxes the muscle around the upper lip so more of the lip can show. Choose filler if your goal is to add volume to either lip.

A lip flip generally wears off sooner than filler. If the muscle relaxes too much, activities such as drinking from a straw can be harder until the effect fades.

## Care for Your Lips After Filler

Swelling often eases in the first couple of days and can take a week to go away. A cold pack used briefly can help with swelling and tenderness.

Wait until any numbing medicine wears off before eating so you don’t accidentally bite your lip. Avoid exercise and skip lipstick and lip balm for at least 24 hours, as advised in the [Cleveland Clinic’s lip filler guide](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22133-lip-fillers).

If you have a fever or redness and swelling around an injection site that get worse, contact your clinician promptly about possible infection.

A rare but serious risk is filler blocking blood flow, which can injure the skin or affect your vision. Get medical help right away for unusual pain, skin near the injection that turns white, gray, or blue, vision changes, or signs of stroke during or shortly after treatment, as advised in the [FDA’s dermal filler safety guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers).

## How Long Will the Change Last?

In separate studies reviewed by the FDA, some people still had fuller lips about a year after treatment with either gel. Hyaluronic acid filler fades at different rates, so you can see how the shape changes before deciding whether to maintain it.

If healed filler leaves a lump or more volume than you wanted, a clinician can assess the area. Hyaluronidase, an enzyme that breaks down hyaluronic acid, may help dissolve some or all of the gel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What’s the Best Lip Filler for a Subtle Result?

Both Restylane Kysse and RHA 3 can add lip fullness. For a subtle change, a clinician can limit the amount and choose where to place it based on whether you want to define the edge, add fullness, or soften a slight difference between the two sides.

### Can Lip Filler Define My Lips Without Making Them Look Much Bigger?

Yes. Filler placed to shape the edge or Cupid’s bow can make the outline clearer. It still adds volume where it’s placed, so the amount matters even when your main goal is definition.

### How Soon Can I Judge My Lip Filler Result?

YAB Aesthetic Institute in Fairfax uses one to two weeks after the last treatment as a general guide. Early swelling can make the lips look bigger or less even than they’ll look once they’ve healed.

### What’s the Difference Between a Lip Flip and Lip Filler?

A lip flip uses Botox to relax the muscle near the upper lip so more of the lip shows. Lip filler adds volume and can shape either lip. A lip flip may suit someone whose upper lip rolls inward when they smile.
