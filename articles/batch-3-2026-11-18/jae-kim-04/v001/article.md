## Match the Treatment to What You Want to Change

Compare how your lips look when your face is relaxed, when you smile, and from the side. A lip that looks thin from the front may already project as far forward as you want from the side.

- **More definition.** If lip liner is how you usually bring out the shape of your lips, talk about the border and the Cupid’s bow, the curve in the middle of the upper lip. Filler can give these features more definition without making overall fullness the main goal.
- **A small increase in fullness.** If your lips look less full than they used to, or you have always wanted more visible lip, filler can add volume to the lip itself. Point out whether you want more fullness in the upper lip, lower lip, or both.
- **Less noticeable unevenness.** Filler can soften some differences in shape or fullness between the two sides. If the difference appears only when you smile, ask the clinician to look at the movement before deciding whether more volume would help.

Dr. Jae Kim’s [guide to filling, flipping, or lifting the lip](https://www.jaekimmd.com/lip-fillers-fairfax-va/) describes separate approaches for adding volume, making more of the upper lip visible, and changing the distance between the nose and upper lip. A lip flip relaxes part of the muscle around the upper lip, allowing it to turn slightly outward without adding volume.

The effect develops over several days. Relaxing those muscles can also make it harder to sip from a straw or control the lips while drinking until the effect wears off.

A surgical lip lift changes the position of the upper lip by removing a small strip of skin beneath the nose. If the distance between your nose and upper lip is your main concern, discuss the potential change, scar, and recovery with a facial plastic surgeon.

## How to Compare Hyaluronic Acid Fillers

YAB’s [dermal filler portfolio](https://www.jaekimmd.com/dermal-fillers/) lists Restylane Kysse and RHA 3. The FDA has approved both for lip augmentation in adults 22 and older.

Ask for the exact product name, since approval applies to the specific gel and use. A plan to define an edge has a different purpose from one designed to make the center of the lips look fuller.

Gels vary in how much support they give and how they move. Ask why the clinician recommends a particular gel for the change you want.

You can also ask whether a small first change and later reassessment make sense. That gives you time to see your lips after the early swelling settles before deciding whether you want more.

A syringe is a package size. Ask how much filler your clinician plans to use and how that amount relates to what you want to change.

## What Treatment and Recovery Feel Like

The practice’s lip filler service describes topical numbing as an option, though you may still feel pressure during the injections. Before you leave, ask what aftercare to follow and how to reach the office if something feels wrong.

Some fullness is visible as soon as filler is placed. Swelling can make the change look stronger at first.

The practice says to assess the result about one to two weeks after the final appointment. If you’re planning around a special event, allow time for visible swelling or bruising to settle.

Mild swelling, tenderness, or bruising can occur after treatment. As the [FDA explains in its dermal filler safety guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/dermal-fillers-soft-tissue-fillers), filler can rarely enter a blood vessel and interrupt blood flow.

Seek immediate medical attention for unusual pain, white, gray, or blue skin near the injection site, or vision changes during or soon after treatment. Stroke symptoms such as sudden facial droop or trouble speaking also need emergency care.

[Hyaluronic acid lip filler fades over time](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22133-lip-fillers). You can let the effect wear off or talk with your clinician about keeping the change.

For an unwanted result, a clinician may be able to break down hyaluronic acid filler with an enzyme called hyaluronidase. Dissolving filler is another medical procedure with its own risks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Lip Filler Define My Lip Border Without Much Added Fullness?

Yes. Filler can make the edge of the lip or the Cupid’s bow more distinct. Tell your clinician you want to keep the overall size close to where it is now, so the plan can focus on definition.

### What’s the Difference Between Lip Filler and a Lip Flip?

Hyaluronic acid lip filler adds volume. A lip flip relaxes part of the muscle around the upper lip so it rolls slightly outward without adding volume. Which makes sense depends on what you want to change.

### When Will I See My Lip Filler Results?

YAB’s lip filler service describes one to two weeks after the final appointment as a reasonable time to see the result. The immediate look can include swelling along with the fullness from filler.

### Is Hyaluronic Acid Lip Filler Permanent?

No. The added fullness fades over time. How long it lasts varies with the product and person, and you can decide with your clinician whether you want maintenance.
