## What Does a Botox Unit Tell You?

A unit measures the activity of the drug. Your injector can use several units at one injection point and can treat one area at several points.

When a practice bills by the unit, it multiplies the total units in your plan by the rate.

Botox Cosmetic and Dysport measure their units differently. When comparing brands, use each plan’s full treatment price for the same concern. Each plan has its own unit count.

The forehead example includes two areas. The label pairs treatment of the horizontal lines with treatment between the brows to help reduce the risk of brow droop.
