## Which Lines Can Botox Soften?

Botox Cosmetic temporarily reduces the pull of the muscles that crease the skin when you make an expression. The FDA has approved it for temporary improvement of moderate to severe frown lines, forehead lines, and crow’s feet in adults. [Botox Cosmetic prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=485d9b71-6881-42c5-a620-a4360c7192ab)

- **Frown lines.** These vertical creases between the eyebrows are often called “11s.” If they deepen when you concentrate or frown, relaxing the muscles that draw the brows together can soften that furrow.
- **Forehead lines.** The horizontal lines that show when you lift your brows come from a muscle that also helps keep the brows raised. The Botox Cosmetic prescribing information calls for treating the forehead and the area between the brows together to lower the risk of a drooping brow.
- **Crow’s feet.** These lines fan out from the outer corners of your eyes when you smile or squint. If those creases are your main concern, your clinician can focus the plan on the muscles around the outer corners of the eyes.

A crease that stays visible with your face relaxed may still soften. For example, some people with deeper frown lines see improvement at rest as well as when they frown.

An etched crease can still show after treatment, even when the muscles underneath pull less. If your main concern is a line at rest, how much it changes when you frown or lift your brows can help your clinician judge how much of it Botox may soften.

## When Will You See Results, and How Long Do They Last?

You may see some softening in the first week, and it can take about two weeks to see the full effect. If you’re planning around a special event, allow time for the result to develop and any bruising to fade.

The effect typically lasts about three to four months. As muscle movement returns, the lines that go with it can become more visible again.
