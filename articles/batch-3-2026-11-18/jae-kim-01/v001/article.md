## Which Wrinkles Respond to Botox?

A line that shows mainly when you make an expression is often a good match for Botox. The medication quiets the muscle activity that folds the skin, so the crease can look softer when you make that same expression.

- **Frown lines** form between the brows when you draw them together. These vertical creases can leave the area looking tense, especially when they deepen with movement.
- **Forehead lines** run across the forehead when you raise your brows.
- **[Crow’s-feet](https://www.jaekimmd.com/botox-for-crows-feet-in-fairfax/)** spread from the outer corners of the eyes when you smile or squint. Treatment can soften the creases around the eyes.

Compare your face in a still photo with a photo of yourself smiling. A crease that deepens when you smile may soften with treatment. A deeper crease that stays visible at rest may still be noticeable afterward.

## What Makes Botox Look Natural?

Your forehead and eyebrows move together. When you raise your brows to open up your expression, the same muscles can crease the skin across your forehead. Relaxing those muscles too much can make the brows sit lower or feel heavy.

A clinician can assess that relationship by looking at your face while it’s relaxed and while you frown, smile, or raise your eyebrows. The movements show what creates each line and whether the brows already move differently on each side.

If you like the way your eyes crinkle when you laugh, say so. You can set a goal of softening the creases at the corners while keeping some of that movement.

## What Can You Expect From Treatment and Recovery?

Before treatment, share your medicines and any recent wrinkle-relaxing injections, including what you received and when. Prescription blood thinners can increase bruising, so discuss them with the clinician who manages them before making any changes.

A licensed clinician uses a fine needle to place small amounts of the prescription medicine into selected muscles. Most people can go back to their usual routine after the visit. Avoid rubbing the treated areas and follow your clinician’s aftercare guidance.

Changes may begin within a few days, and it can take a week or longer to see the full effect. If you’re planning around photos or a special event, allow time for the result to develop and for any bruising to fade. The softening often lasts about three to four months, and the lines can gradually return as muscle activity comes back.

[Temporary soreness, swelling, or bruising](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/botox/about/pac-20384658) can happen. Headaches and a drooping brow or eyelid are also possible, so contact your clinician if an eyelid starts to droop. The medication can rarely affect muscles beyond the treated area. Get medical help right away if you develop trouble breathing, swallowing, or speaking after treatment.
