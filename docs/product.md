# Content Production

## Purpose and Scope

Enable Vikas to fulfill Benchmark Medical Partners’ assigned long-form SEO and AEO writing work.

The product starts with supplied article assignments and covers their research, drafting, evaluation, revision, and customer delivery. Alex’s keyword research, his third-party research tools and data workflows, and decisions about which articles to commission remain outside this version’s scope.

## Goals

- Produce articles that meet [Writing Standards](writing-standards.md) and match the quality of Benchmark’s writers. [Alex’s review and acceptance](userdesign.md#review-revision-and-writing-feedback) determine whether the delivered articles meet the customer’s quality expectations. Meeting measurable requirements alone does not establish article quality.
- Deliver articles directly to Alex without requiring Vikas to review or approve each article, under [User Design](userdesign.md#assignment-delivery-and-tracking).
- Give Vikas confidence that completed articles meet their applicable requirements, supported by [article verification](engineering.md#article-verification).

## Assignment Scope

The initial workload comprises 52 long-form SEO and AEO articles: 22 for DC Derm Docs, 22 for La-Mon’e Aesthetics, and eight for Estar MedSpa.
