2026 SEO Blogging & Content Writing Standards

## Our Goal

We are not writing for Google. We are writing for people first and optimizing for search engines second. Every article should:

- Answer the reader's question
- Demonstrate expertise (Mention providers/credentials)
- Build trust (Mention practice/credentials)
- Improve user experience
- Support rankings organically
- Encourage action

If a blog is useful, engaging, and well-structured, it will typically outperform content written solely around keyword density or keyword stuffing.

## Content Philosophy

Before writing, ask yourself: "What question is the reader trying to answer?" This is generally given to you. Then create content that answers that question clearly, accurately, and thoroughly. Google's Helpful Content System rewards content that demonstrates real expertise and provides value to readers. This includes specifics on the practice/provider you’re writing for.

## Article Length

### Standard Blog Posts

- 600–900 words

### Competitive Topics

- 900–1,500 words

### Local SEO Landing Pages

- 600–1,200 words

Never add fluff simply to reach a word count goal.

## Writing Style

### Write Like a Human

**Use:**

- Conversational language
- Active voice
- Short paragraphs
- Natural transitions
- Clear explanations
- Real-world examples when appropriate

**Avoid:**

- Keyword stuffing
- Repetitive wording
- Generic AI phrases
- Overly technical language unless appropriate
- Long walls of text
- Excessive bolding of keywords and keyword phrases

## Introduction Framework

Use the APP Formula whenever possible.

### Agree

Show readers you understand their problem.

### Promise

Introduce a solution.

### Preview

Tell them what they will learn.

**Example**

If you're considering Botox for the first time, you're probably wondering how long results last and whether the treatment is worth it. The good news is that Botox can provide noticeable improvement with minimal downtime. In this guide, we'll explain how Botox works, how long results typically last, and what factors influence longevity.

## Headings

Use descriptive, benefit-driven headings that answer questions readers actually have.

**Weak Heading**

Benefits of Botox

**Better Heading**

How Botox Helps Reduce Fine Lines and Wrinkles

**Weak Heading**

Recovery

**Better Heading**

What to Expect After Botox Treatment

Headings should be useful, specific, and reader-focused.

## SEO Keyword Strategy

### Primary Keyword

Include naturally in:

- Title
- First 100 words
- At least one H2 heading
- Meta title
- Meta description
- URL (when applicable)

Do not force a specific keyword count. Google understands context and search intent better than ever.

## Supporting Keywords

Include related terms naturally throughout the content.

**Example**

**Primary Keyword:**

Botox in Rockville, MD

**Related Keywords (LSI Keywords):**

- wrinkle relaxers
- facial rejuvenation
- cosmetic injections
- frown lines
- crow's feet
- forehead lines

These supporting terms help build topical relevance. Essentially, what would help a reader and search engines understand the context of the piece of content.

## Internal Linking

Every article should include two-to-four internal links (two for a 300 word article, four for a 600 or more word article). This does not include a call to action link (Contact us, our team etc.).

**Priority order:**

1. Main service page
2. Related service pages
3. Relevant blog articles

**Example**

**Botox blog:**

- Botox
- Dermal Fillers
- Wrinkle Relaxers
- Medical Spa Services

The first internal link should typically point to the primary service being promoted unless it is in the keyword/keyword phrase. Do not link the same internal link twice.

## External Linking

Include one-to-two external links when appropriate.

**Preferred sources:**

- American Academy of Dermatology
- Mayo Clinic
- NIH
- CDC
- FDA
- American Society of Plastic Surgeons

Do not link to direct competitors or directories such as Wikipedia.

## EEAT Standards

Google increasingly prioritizes:

### Experience

Demonstrate familiarity with patient concerns and expectations.

### Expertise

Provide accurate, medically sound information.

### Authoritativeness

Reference trusted information and established best practices.

### Trustworthiness

Avoid exaggerated claims and unsupported promises.

## Medical Content Rules

**Never:**

- Guarantee results
- Promise outcomes
- Claim a treatment is risk-free
- Use unsupported statistics
- Create unrealistic expectations

**Instead use:**

- "Many patients experience..."
- "Results vary from person to person."
- "A consultation can determine whether you're a candidate."

- "Most patients notice..."

## Formatting Standards

### Paragraphs

Keep paragraphs short. Most should be 1–2 sentences.

### Lists

Use bullets whenever possible to improve readability.

### Sections

Break content into logical sections using H2 headings.

### Readability

**Target:**

- 6th–9th grade reading level
- Easy scanning
- Short sentences

## AP Style Requirements

All content should follow Associated Press (AP) Style unless a client specifically requests otherwise.

### Numbers

Spell out numbers one through nine. Use numerals for 10 and above.

**Examples**

- three appointments
- seven treatments
- 12 patients
- 25 years

### States

Use custom abbreviations when appropriate.

**Examples**

- Bethesda, MD.
- Arlington, VA.
- Fairfax, VA.
- Washington, DC

### Percentages

Use the percent symbol.

**Example**

- 25% improvement

### Professional Titles

Capitalize professional titles only when they appear before a person's name.

**Examples**

- Dr. Jane Smith
- Jane Smith, MD

### Dates

**Examples**

- June 15, 2026
- Monday, June 15

### Oxford Comma

BMP uses the Oxford comma for consistency.

**Example**

- Botox, fillers, and laser treatments

### Hyphenation

Follow AP Style guidelines for compound modifiers.

**Examples**

- long-term results
- state-of-the-art technology
- minimally invasive procedure

### Phone Numbers

Always format phone numbers as: 301-555-1234

### Headline Capitalization

Use AP headline-style capitalization for all headings.

**Helpful resource:**

<https://capitalizemytitle.com/>

Select AP Style before generating headings.

## Local SEO Requirements

When applicable, naturally include:

- City
- State
- Service area

**Example**

Patients seeking Botox in Rockville, MD, often ask how long treatment results last. Do not force geographic keywords unnaturally.

## Calls to Action

Every article should end with a clear call to action and link to scheduling page and/or phone number.

**Example**

**Ready to Learn More About Botox?**

If you're interested in smoother, more youthful-looking skin, call 123-456-8999 to schedule a consultation with ABC Medical Spa. Our experienced providers can create a personalized treatment plan based on your goals and concerns.

## AI Usage Policy

AI may be used for:

- Research assistance
- Topic brainstorming
- Outlines
- Draft generation
- Check phone numbers

Every article must still be:

- Fact checked
- Edited
- Human reviewed
- Customized for the client

Never submit AI-generated content without substantial editing and verification.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Repeating the same keyword excessively
- Writing introductions that immediately start selling
- Using vague headings
- Making unsupported claims
- Linking to competitors
- Creating long paragraphs
- Using generic AI phrases such as:
  - "In today's fast-paced world"
  - "When it comes to"
  - "Unlock the secrets"
  - "Delve into"
  - "Game-changing"
- Copying content from competitors
