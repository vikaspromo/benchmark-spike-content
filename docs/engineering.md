# Engineering

Engineering owns implementation discipline and verification. It defines how product changes are developed, investigated, and reviewed, and which methods and evidence establish that articles and product behavior satisfy their requirements.

[Product](product.md) owns the required outcomes and scope; [Writing Standards](writing-standards.md) owns article requirements; [User Design](userdesign.md) owns the operator and customer experience. [Architecture](architecture.md#evaluate-and-revise) owns the single article evaluation and revision flow; Engineering defines how to verify its requirements without adding editorial obligations or another revision flow. [Data Model](data-model.md#assignments-and-article-status) owns assessment records. [Release](release.md) owns product deployment and recovery; [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) owns agent responsibilities and governing-document changes.

## Article Verification

Establish whether the resulting article meets [Writing Standards](writing-standards.md), applicable assignment-specific instructions, and [User Design's presentation requirements](userdesign.md#article-content-and-writing-standards). Those documents own the requirements; verification does not add editorial obligations. [Architecture](architecture.md#evaluate-and-revise) owns when checking and revision occur.

Use a checklist covering every applicable requirement, with its conditions and exceptions intact. Articles must be fact checked, edited, and substantially verified before delivery; never deliver AI-generated content without substantial editing and verification. AI may assist research, brainstorming, outlining, drafting, and phone checks. Mechanical checks may establish counts, formatting, and other directly measurable properties. Editorial assessment must establish reader usefulness, heading relevance, distinct coverage, voice, and meaningful practice customization under Writing Standards. Evidence assessment must establish factual support, correct associations, and justified medical qualifications. Complete grammar and spelling checks and a final proofread against Writing Standards, including its voice, reader fit, and writing conventions. A passed count or supported claim alone does not establish editorial quality.

Verify [Clinician Selection](writing-standards.md#clinician-selection) by counting the clinicians featured against the stated limit and checking the evidence for their experience, expertise, treatment involvement, and attributed approach. Assess whether the article gives the reader a supported reason to consider each clinician for the concern discussed.

Verify [Readability](writing-standards.md#readability) by counting prose body paragraphs and the sentences in each. Exclude headings, list items, captions, and comments; account for abbreviations and initials when identifying sentence boundaries. Record the number and proportion of body paragraphs containing one or two sentences in the final assessment. Review whether parallel information would be clearer as bullets, and identify passages that need that change.

Verify [Reader Priorities](writing-standards.md#reader-priorities), [Article Voice](writing-standards.md#article-voice), and [Section Focus](writing-standards.md#section-focus) by reading the completed article section by section and across sections. Establish the question or subject stated by each heading, then check whether each paragraph, bullet, and example develops that answer through facts, explanations, comparisons, examples, or necessary qualifications. Identify useful passages that belong under another heading and sections that overlap without adding distinct value. For first-time treatment articles, check whether the relevant sequence is understandable and whether the evidence supports any stated practice process or visit timing. Assess whether important facts are connected to their supported practical meaning and whether the language sounds natural. Identify passages that give advice instead of an available answer, repeat a point without developing it, or add generic warnings after the relevant limitation is already clear. Stock-phrase searches and paragraph counts cannot establish these results.

Support each checklist result with evidence appropriate to the requirement. For editorial results, point to representative passages in the exact article and explain how they satisfy the writing requirement. A list of present facts, links, qualifications, or clinician names is not enough to establish reader usefulness. Record concrete violations as failures with their locations and needed corrections; do not mark the requirement passed while leaving those violations as editorial caveats. Retain concise evidence in the final-check portion of the support-and-check record under [Data Model](data-model.md#assignments-and-article-status).

Use source evidence to establish factual claims and included images or captions. For the practice-substitution test under [Customization](writing-standards.md#customization), identify actual content or images that need substantive rewriting or replacement for another practice offering the same services. Distinguish that evidence from the article's general educational value; passing substitution alone does not establish useful explanations.

Verify each caption under [Images](writing-standards.md#images): check whether it explains a supported point the image illustrates, connects to the nearby content, and includes limits needed for accurate interpretation. Inspect the rendered article to establish image legibility, placement, proportions, caption presentation, and preservation of meaning under [User Design](userdesign.md#article-content-and-writing-standards). Planned use or a source URL does not prove that an image is present. Do not report an unavailable input or an unperformed check as a pass.

Use [Data Model's assessment distinctions](data-model.md#assignments-and-article-status) for passes, failures, required facts awaiting Alex, optional requests, and requirements that do not apply. An absent optional feature does not itself establish failure. Measure body words using Writing Standards' range and counting scope, and record the count in the assessment rather than the customer article.

Verify the exact resulting article version identified in the support-and-check record. Findings guide [Architecture's single evaluation and revision flow](architecture.md#evaluate-and-revise). Update the record with the final recheck outcome rather than retain separate correction reports. [Data Model](data-model.md#assignments-and-article-status) defines its contents and retention limits.

Before claiming delivery complete, verify that the actual customer Doc preserves the evaluated article and required comments under [Architecture](architecture.md#deliver-to-alex). Confirm that each image is embedded inline, its caption appears immediately below it, and the caption's Google Doc comment contains the correct source links under [User Design](userdesign.md#article-content-and-writing-standards). Inspect the delivered image's legibility and proportions. Local checks establish only the local result. If delivery changes content or presentation, assess the resulting version through Architecture's evaluation flow.

Keep local image files only while needed to create or verify the article. Remove those files when that need ends; a separate original-image archive is not required for delivery or proof of completion. Keep the source references and supporting context under [Data Model](data-model.md#article-research-material).

Verification supports direct delivery without Vikas's article-by-article approval. It does not establish customer acceptance or assessment reliability across assignments. [User Design](userdesign.md#review-revision-and-writing-feedback) defines Alex's review and acceptance.
