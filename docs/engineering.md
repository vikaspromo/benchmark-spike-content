# Engineering

Engineering owns implementation discipline and verification. It defines how content production product changes are implemented, investigated and reviewed, and what evidence establishes their correctness.

Read the sections governing the change and the claims being made. Product owns system purpose and scope; User Design owns product-user behavior, language and voice; Writing Standards owns article content and writing standards; Data Model owns record meanings, identity and validity; Architecture owns component responsibilities, processing and serving; Release owns deployment and recovery; AGENTS owns agent responsibilities, authorization and governing-document changes.

Engineering uses those governing requirements to establish implementation and verification expectations. It does not independently define product behavior or deployment procedures.

## Article Verification

Before delivery, fact check and substantially edit each article, and verify its quality and client customization against [Writing Standards](writing-standards.md). That document owns the active writing requirements, applicability, and approved exceptions. Verification must support direct delivery without requiring Vikas to review or approve individual articles.

Verify the content, SEO, formatting, and quality-control requirements in [Editing, Verification, and Human Review](writing-standards.md#editing-verification-and-human-review). Preserve each requirement's conditions and overrides. For conditional material, record relevance, availability, and disposition. Distinguish failed requirements, missing required inputs, unavailable optional material, and requirements that do not apply. Do not fail an article merely because an optional feature is absent or report an unavailable required input as a passed check.

Assess compliance, factual support, and reader usefulness separately. Counts, formatting checks, and supported claims do not establish that the article explains the topic well. For reader usefulness, identify what the intended reader learns from each section, whether every paragraph, bullet, and example directly supports its heading, and whether the section adds information beyond earlier sections. Identify tangents, duplicated answers, and passages that can be removed without losing useful information. Assess whether repetition serves a clear purpose and whether consultation advice substitutes for an answer the research can support.

Apply [Writing Standards' practice-substitution test](writing-standards.md#practice-research-and-provider-coverage) to the complete article. Identify the substantive passages that would need factual rewriting for another practice, the practice evidence supporting them, and their contribution to the reader's understanding or consultation choice. Interchangeable names, credentials, locations, and links do not establish a pass. General explanations may remain reusable; no percentage threshold applies. If no substantive passage passes the test, record failed customization and return the article for further practice research and revision under Architecture.

Record editorial defects with their locations or quoted passages, why they weaken the reader's understanding, and the correction required. Correct failed writing requirements and repeat affected assessments on the resulting version under [Architecture](architecture.md#evaluate-and-revise). Preserve justified medical qualifications and safety advice when cutting repetition. Do not use a passed mechanical check to dismiss an unresolved editorial defect. Distinguish optional improvements from failures under Writing Standards.

Record the final body-word count and whether it meets the range and counting scope in [Writing Standards](writing-standards.md#reader-answer-and-substantive-content). Record this measurement in the production assessment, not in the customer-facing article. Verify that revisions made to meet the range preserve useful coverage, factual qualifications, and meaningful practice details. A count within the range does not excuse a quality failure.

Verification does not establish customer acceptance or the reliability of the assessment method across assignments. [User Design](userdesign.md#review-revision-and-writing-feedback) defines Alex's review and acceptance.
